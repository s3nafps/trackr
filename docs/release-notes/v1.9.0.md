## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.9.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Seasons watched**: for a TV show with several seasons, the status sheet now has a row of season chips. Tap **S1** if you watched the first season and dropped it, **S3** if you got through three, and the episode count fills in for you. Tap the last chip again to undo it.
- It also works when you add a show from **Search** or the **Home** bookmark. Before, only shows already in your list knew their seasons.
- For the season you're partway through, a stepper counts its episodes. Stepping past the last episode finishes the season.
- Choosing **Completed** marks every season as watched.
- Anime still counts episodes: each season of an anime is its own title, so you add the seasons you watched one by one.
