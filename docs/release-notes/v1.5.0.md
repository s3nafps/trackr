Friends' activity, reactions and comments now appear as they happen.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.5.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Live friends feed**: what your friends watch, their reactions and comments, recommendations and friend requests show up while you're on the Friends tab, without pulling to refresh.
- **Live comments**: an open comments sheet shows new comments as they're posted.
- **Live shared watchlists**: titles and members your friends add appear right away.
- On a title you've tracked, friends' reactions to it update live too. Live updates pause when Trackr is in the background, and anything missed loads when you come back.
