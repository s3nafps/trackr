Trackr now tells you when there's a new version.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.4.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Update check**: when a newer version is published here, Trackr shows what's new and a **Download** button. Choose **Later** and it won't ask again about that version. It checks at most twice a day.
- **Check for updates** in Profile → About, with the version you have.
