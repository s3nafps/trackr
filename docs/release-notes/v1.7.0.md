Track TV shows season by season, and see what's coming up.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.7.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Season progress**: shows with several seasons now read "S2 · E5" on Home, in My List and on the title page, and the edit sheet has a Season and an Episode stepper. Finishing a season's last episode moves you on to the next season. Your existing progress converts automatically.
- **Upcoming episodes calendar**: tap the calendar icon in My List to see the next episodes of everything you're watching, grouped by day (Today, Tomorrow, then the date), with air times where the source has them.
- New-episode alerts and the widget now say which season ("Season 2, episode 3 is out").
