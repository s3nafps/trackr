The first public release of Trackr: track the movies, TV shows and anime you watch, together with your friends.

## Install
1. Download **`trackr-v1.0.0.apk`** below on your Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer).
2. Open it and allow installing from your browser or file manager when Android asks.
3. Sign in with Google. While the app is in testing, only Google accounts added as test users can sign in. Ask the person who shared Trackr with you to add yours.

## What's in it
**Tracking**
- Discover: continue watching, trending movies/TV/anime, and what's airing this week.
- Search across TMDB and AniList with filters and recent searches.
- Title pages with **trailers**, **where to watch** in your country (streaming, free, rent, buy), cast, seasons, **related anime** (prequels, sequels, side stories) and **more like this**.
- My List with Watching / Completed / Plan to Watch / Dropped, ratings, episode progress and a quick +1. Works offline and syncs across devices.
- New-episode alerts for shows you watch.

**Your data**
- **Import** from AniList (by username), MyAnimeList (export file) and Letterboxd (export zip), and **export** your list as a JSON backup or a CSV spreadsheet.
- **Year in review**: what you finished this year, your top rated and your busiest month, as an image you can share.
- Account deletion in Settings.

**With friends**
- Add friends by username or invite code and follow their activity.
- **React and comment** on what your friends are watching.
- **Recommend** a title to a friend, and see what's been recommended to you.
- **Shared watchlists** you build together, with **Pick for us** to choose tonight's title, plus **Pick one** from the titles you and a friend both plan to watch.

**Everywhere**
- An **"Up next" home-screen widget**: what's airing this week and what you're watching, with +1.
- Shared links to a title open it straight in Trackr. On Android 12+, turn this on under Settings → *Open links in Trackr*.

## Notes
- Movie and TV data comes from TMDB, and anime from AniList. Streaming availability is provided by JustWatch.
- Titles you finished before this version count toward Year in review by the date you last edited them.
