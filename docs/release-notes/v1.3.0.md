Recommendations made for you, and genres to narrow things down.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.3.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **For You** on Home: picks based on the titles you rated 7 or higher (and the ones you've finished or are watching), with a "Because you liked …" line. Anything already in your list is left out, and the row updates as you rate more.
- **Genres** in Discover More: choose Comedy, Horror, Sci-Fi, Romance and more, together with Movies / TV Shows / Anime. Only genres that exist for the chosen type are shown.
- **Genres in Search**: with an empty search box, a genre shows that genre's popular titles; while searching, it narrows the results.
