## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.1.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over v1.0.0 and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Discover More** on Home: an endless grid of popular movies, TV shows and anime under the trending rows. Use the chips to show only movies, TV shows or anime.
- The **trending rows** on Home keep loading more titles as you swipe to the end.
- **Search** results and the **Trending now** list keep loading as you scroll, instead of stopping after the first 20.
- If loading more fails (for example, you're offline), a **Retry** button appears at the end of the list.
