The top of the app now belongs to the page.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.15.1.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **The bar blends in**: Home, Search, My List, Friends, and Profile no longer sit under a fixed strip. The title is part of the page, on the same colour as the rest of the screen.
- **Your picture is in the header**: the profile photo sits beside the greeting or the screen title, next to the streak and the other actions. Tap it to open your profile.
- **A compact title as you scroll**: once the page moves, a short title and a thin line ease in so you still know where you are. At the top of the page, that line is gone.
