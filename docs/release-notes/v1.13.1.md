Fixes for your profile and your list after signing back in.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.13.1.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Your list comes back when you sign in**: signing out clears the list from the phone. Signing back in now brings it straight back from your account instead of waiting for the next sync.
- **Profile loads again**: your invite code shows again, and changing your username works.
