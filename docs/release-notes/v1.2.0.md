## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.2.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Add to List** in Search now opens the status sheet: pick **Watching**, **Completed**, **Plan to Watch** or **Dropped**, and optionally a rating and episode progress, before it's saved. Titles are no longer added straight to Plan to Watch.
- The same sheet opens from the **bookmark** on posters on Home (trending rows and Discover More).
- Tap the **status badge** on a search result that's already in your list to change its status, rating or progress.
