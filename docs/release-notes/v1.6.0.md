See which genres you watch most.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.6.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Top Genres** on your profile: the genres of the titles you're watching or have completed, as a chart with percentages.
- Genres for titles already in your list are filled in automatically in the background over the first few minutes (it says "so far" until it's done). New titles get them as soon as they're added or opened.
