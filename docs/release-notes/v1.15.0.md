A cleaner look, in dark and light.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.15.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **New look**: Trackr is quieter. Dark and light both use a near-black or near-white canvas, thin borders, and one blue accent. Posters stay the colour on the screen.
- **New app icon**: the launcher icon is a simple play mark, and it follows your themed icons.
- **Loading shimmer**: posters and list rows show a soft sweep while they load, instead of a flat grey box.
- **Smoother moves**: switching tabs fades, and cards ease in when you press them.
- **Your colours stay put**: Android wallpaper theming no longer recolors Trackr.
- Everything you already do is the same: your list, friends, sync, and sign-in are unchanged.
