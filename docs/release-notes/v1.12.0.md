The home-screen widget now leads with what to watch next.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.12.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Up next leads with what to watch**: the widget shows the title to watch next, with its status, a progress bar and the +1 for the next episode.
- **Progress at a glance**: the bar shows each episode as watched, out but not yet watched, or still to come.
- **Also in progress**: your other titles in progress are listed under the hero on the wide widget, each with its own +1.
- **Coming up**: this week's episodes sit beside the hero on the wide widget. On the small widget, the hero shows alone.
