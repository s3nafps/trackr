Home and Search work offline.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.8.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Offline Home and Search**: what you've browsed is saved on your phone. With no connection (or a very bad one), Home and Search show what you saw last instead of empty placeholders, with a small "You're offline" note. Pull down to try again.
- Searches you've done before work offline too.
