The widget knows when you're caught up.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.10.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Caught up on a show?** The widget no longer offers the next episode before it's out. It shows when the next one airs, like **"S5 E1 · Next Wed"**, or **"Caught up"** when nothing is announced yet. The +1 button hides until a new episode is out.
- **Coming next week** now reads as "Next Wed" instead of a date. Further out it shows the date.
- Shows with several seasons read by season in the widget too: **"Next: S2 E6"**.
- Trackr checks shows and anime you're caught up on once a day, so a new episode appears in the widget soon after it airs.
