Two fixes that keep your list safe.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.13.2.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **Signing out offline asks first**: if some changes haven't reached your account yet, Trackr now tells you instead of signing out and losing them.
- **Big lists sync in full**: lists over 1,000 titles now download completely, on your phone and on friends' profiles.
