Smaller fixes and a few things you asked for.

## Install
Download **`trackr-v1.11.0.apk`** below and open it on your phone. It installs over earlier 1.x versions and keeps your list and sign-in.

## What's new
- **+1 stops at what's out**: +1 Ep in My List and Home won't go past the episodes that have aired, so announced episodes can't be marked watched early.
- **Add the next season**: a finished or ongoing anime with a sequel shows "Add next season to your list" on its page, which opens the status sheet for that season.
- **Title pages work offline**: a title you've opened once opens again without a connection.
- **Add to calendar**: an upcoming episode can be added to your phone's calendar, from its title page or from the calendar screen.
- **Update in the app**: Download fetches the new version with the phone's downloader and opens the installer when it's done. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from Trackr.
